Welcome to the documentation home of Rigiity, a volunteer-driven tech and media organization building for longevity.

Everything here is public on purpose: we believe a good digital life should not be hard to have, and that includes showing how we work.

## Where to go

- **[Organization Handbook](/handbook/welcome/overview)**: how Rigiity operates. Start here if you want to volunteer or understand what we expect from the people who build with us.
- **[Products](/products/index)**: documentation, tutorials, and guides for our released software and games.
- **[Guides & Tutorials](/guides/index)**: practical, cross-cutting guides for the tools and practices we use.

Our mission and philosophy live on [our website](https://rigiity.com). Questions or feedback? Reach us through [our contact page](https://rigiity.com/contact/), and report security issues privately per [our security page](https://rigiity.com/security/).
