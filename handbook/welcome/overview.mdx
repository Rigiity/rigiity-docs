This handbook is the operating manual for Rigiity. It explains how we work, what we expect from volunteers, and how to get involved. Our mission and philosophy live on [our website](https://rigiity.com); this handbook covers the practical side.

## Who this is for

- **New volunteers**: start with [Account Setup](/handbook/welcome/account-setup), then read the sections relevant to what you will work on.
- **Current volunteers**: use it as the reference for procedures and standards. If reality and this handbook disagree, either fix reality or fix the handbook.
- **Everyone else**: everything here is public on purpose. If you are curious about getting involved, reading this is the best way to see how we actually operate.

## Pace and availability

Rigiity is volunteer-built in people's free time. Our pace is the sustainable one: work moves when maintainers have the time and energy to do it well, and we'd rather ship something solid late than something rushed on schedule. That trade is deliberate, and it's why our releases hold up.

What you can always expect from us: honesty about where things stand, and urgency when it truly matters. A critical vulnerability or a security patch for something people depend on gets handled as soon as possible, because reliability is our promise.

<Note>
Volunteers' free time stays theirs. We don't pressure each other about pace, and we ask the same of everyone else: patience is part of the price of free.
</Note>

### Not everything is work

Rigiity is built by people who like each other's company, and that shows outside of projects too. We occasionally host informal digital activities just for fun or relaxation: a game server for part of the year, or whatever else sounds good at the time, within reasonable funding arrangements.

These are optional in every sense. Nobody is expected to show up, and nobody is evaluated by whether they do. They're also not a service we promise; they happen when someone feels like making them happen, same as everything else here.

## How it is organized

- **Welcome & Onboarding**: getting set up and how we communicate.
- **Tech Protocols**: coding standards, server access, deployment safety, and security.
- **Operational Procedures**: how projects are proposed, resources requested, and tasks move through our boards.
- **Offboarding**: what happens when projects wind down or volunteers move on.
- **Legal & Licensing**: our legal positions on licensing, enforcement, and referencing others.

## How it is maintained

Changes to this handbook go through pull requests to [our docs repository](https://github.com/Rigiity/rigiity-docs), reviewed and merged by management or an approved reviewer. If something here is wrong or unclear, that is a bug in our documentation, and we want to hear about it.
