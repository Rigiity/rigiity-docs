---
title: "Mentioning other companies"
description: "How we reference other companies' names and brands."
---

Naming companies and products in plain text to state facts is fine: everyone writes "Apple" or "GitHub" when the fact calls for it. The rules that keep that safe:

- Use the name only as much as the point requires.
- Don't imply endorsement or affiliation.
- Don't use their logos except as pointers to our own profiles on their platforms, unmodified and less prominent than our own branding. Anything decorative or altered: check that brand's guidelines first.
- When a use feels borderline, plain text plus a "trademarks belong to their respective owners" line is the safe shape.
