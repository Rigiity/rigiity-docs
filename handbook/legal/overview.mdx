It exists because nothing public-facing ships without review, and a reviewer needs a canonical reference to review against. Volunteers who want to understand the positions behind that review can read them here, anywhere, anytime.

- [Licensing of our work](/handbook/legal/licensing-of-our-work)
- [Enforcement stance](/handbook/legal/enforcement-stance)
