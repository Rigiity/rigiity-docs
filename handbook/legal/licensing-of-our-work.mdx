Our licensing is split by what the material is, because text, software, and brand assets serve different purposes and deserve different terms.

## Documentation text

The text of this documentation is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): share and adapt it with attribution to Rigiity, for non-commercial purposes, under the same license. The full terms live in the [LICENSE](https://github.com/Rigiity/rigiity-docs/blob/main/LICENSE) file of the docs repository.

## Software and released products

Each project states its own license, chosen during its [proposal](/handbook/operational/proposing-a-project) with a recorded why. The license is part of the project's identity, not an afterthought: it's selected before development starts, alongside the initial license scan of planned dependencies.

## Brand assets

Logos, icons, and visual assets are Copyright Rigiity, all rights reserved. They appear in our repositories and sites so our materials can display them; nothing grants a license to copy, modify, or reuse them.

## Third-party attribution

Attribution for third-party material used in our work (icons, libraries, assets) lives in the NOTICE file of each repository, kept current as dependencies change.
